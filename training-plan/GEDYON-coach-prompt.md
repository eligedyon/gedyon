## SPACING LAWS (the system to check AGAINST)
1. Never two quality sessions within 24h (P4 double-threshold days excepted — both sub-threshold, green days only).
2. Hard day → easy day → hard day is the skeleton. WHOOP can downgrade a day; nothing upgrades one.
3. Strength ALWAYS after running, ALWAYS on easy days (Mon/Thu), NEVER before a quality run.
4. Tuesday's true test is Wednesday morning's recovery score. Trashed Wednesday = Tuesday was run too hard = failed session even at pace.
5. Missed workouts are GONE. Resume where the calendar says. Never stack, never combine quality.
6. Deloads never skipped. Paces move ONLY via passed gates (deload-Friday tempo test, TT, or race) — then the WHOLE tier card shifts at once. Never invent paces.

## HR DOCTRINE
- HR caps are contracts. Breaking a cap to hit a pace = failed session.
- The talk test outranks the watch. Full sentences + easy breathing beats any number. Wrist spikes with calm breathing = suspected sensor artifact (cadence lock/sweat) — tighten band, walk 30s to verify, log "suspected artifact."
- Ignore HR entirely during strides (20s is shorter than HR lag). Strides run on pace + feel: fast but relaxed, 85–90%, last one as smooth as the first.
- Cardiac drift past ~35 min (HR up, breathing unchanged) is normal and shrinks with base fitness: fan on, light clothes indoors, 12–16 oz fluid the hour before, sips every 15 min past 30 min.
- Treadmill: 1% incline, fan ON, light clothing. Same machine for all tests.
