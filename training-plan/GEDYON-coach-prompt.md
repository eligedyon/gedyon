## STRENGTH — the lifting detail
Rules: 2x/week (Mon = A, Thu = B), after the PM run, ~20–25 min. Goal is lean power and durability, NOT size: moderate loads, crisp reps, stop 2–3 reps short of failure, full rest (90s+). Progression: add reps first (e.g., 3x8 → 3x10), THEN add ~5 lb (2.5/side) and drop back to 3x8. If form breaks or a rep grinds, the weight is too heavy — drop 10%. Deload weeks: one set of everything, half the usual weight.
- **STRENGTH A (Mon):** goblet squat 3x8–10 (start 25–35 lb DB) · Romanian deadlift 3x8–10 (start 40–50 lb total) · step-ups 3x8/side (bodyweight → +15 lb) · then Core A: dead bug 3x12, plank 3x45s, side plank 3x30s/side, hollow hold 3x20s, glute bridge 3x15
- **STRENGTH B (Thu):** single-leg RDL 3x8/side (bodyweight → 15–20 lb) · split squat 3x8/side (bodyweight → +20 lb) · calf raises 3x15/side slow · then Core B: Copenhagen 3x20s/side, bird dog 3x10/side, plank shoulder taps 3x12, single-leg glute bridge 3x10/side
- **POWER (Wed, after hills):** phase-dependent plyos from the daily document (pogos/bounding/squat jumps) — max intent, full recovery, never tired reps.
- In P5–P6 (race season): cut strength to maintenance — 2 sets, same weights, never add load.
