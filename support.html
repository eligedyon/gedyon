Support

Need help with GEDYON or KODA? Email eligedyon@gmail.com and you will get a reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Cloud Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data from our servers. If it fails, email us and we will delete it within 48 hours.

How do I cancel KODA Unlimited?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GEDYON. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I bought KODA Unlimited but it is not unlocked

Open KODA, wait for the upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same GEDYON account and Apple ID.

How do I stop GEDYON reading Apple Health?

iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → GEDYON, then turn off the categories you want.

How do I turn off AI?

Settings → Legal & Support, untick "Allow KODA to send my data to Anthropic". Nothing is sent to the AI while it is off.
