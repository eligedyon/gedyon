Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

GEDYON is an AI-assisted training app made by Elias Gedyon ("we", "us"), an independent developer in Colorado, USA. This policy explains what data GEDYON collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Questions: eligedyon@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what you enter or choose to connect, to run your training plan and your AI coach, KODA.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • KODA is powered by Anthropic's Claude. Your data goes to Anthropic only after you allow it in the app, and only to generate KODA's replies.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never written back to Apple Health.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Delete account.

If you use GEDYON without an account, your data stays in your browser or on your device and is not sent to our servers, except what you send to KODA after allowing AI.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers below, only as needed to run GEDYON, and each is bound by its own terms to protect it at least as well as this policy does:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Anthropic (Claude): generates KODA's replies and meal-photo analysis. When you use an AI feature we send your message, any photo you attach, and relevant profile, training, nutrition and health data you have logged or synced. This happens only after you tap Allow on the in-app AI permission screen; you can turn it off in Settings → Legal & Support. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train Anthropic's models and is kept only for a limited period.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: process and verify subscriptions.
  • Strava or WHOOP: only if you connect them.
  • Hosting providers (GitHub Pages or Netlify) that serve the web app.

We may disclose data if required by law. We do not use health or fitness data, including Apple Health data, for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above to provide your coaching.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, logs, health data, KODA history and usage records are deleted from our database right away; copies in our providers' backups expire within about 30 days. Subscription records held by Apple are governed by Apple.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. If that fails, email us and we will delete it within 48 hours.
  • Revoke AI permission: Settings → Legal & Support, untick the AI permission. The rest of the app keeps working.
  • Revoke Apple Health access: iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → GEDYON.
  • Export your data: Settings → Data → Export.
  • Access or correct your data: edit it in the app or email us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your data; email us to use them.

Children

GEDYON is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and each user's records are protected by row-level access rules so that only that user can read them. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you find a problem.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and tell you in the app about significant changes.
